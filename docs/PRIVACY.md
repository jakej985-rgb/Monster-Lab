# Monster Lab - Privacy Policy & Child Safety Statement

**Last Updated:** September 29, 2026
**Target Audience:** Parents, Educators, and Young Learners (Ages 8–14)

---

## 1. Our Core Principle: 100% Offline & Private by Design

Monster Lab was built from the ground up to be safe for children. The application functions completely offline and does not connect to external servers, cloud databases, or third-party tracking services during ordinary educational use.

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Monster Lab does **NOT** collect, request, process, or transmit any of the following:
- Real names, home addresses, phone numbers, or email addresses
- Passwords or account credentials
- Geolocation data or IP addresses
- Audio, camera, or biometric recordings
- Analytics, behavioral metrics, or user profiling telemetry
- Device identifiers or advertising IDs

---

## 3. Local Data Storage

All data generated during application usage is stored strictly on the local device using sandboxed local storage (`SharedPreferences` / application sandboxed storage):
- **Learner Profiles**: Fictional explorer nicknames (e.g., "Sparky") and chosen monster avatars.
- **Progress Records**: Completed lessons, projects built, components discovered, quiz answers, and XP points.
- **Safety Preferences**: Parent toggles for physical sensor unlocking.

Parents and educators can export this data at any time as a plaintext JSON file or clear it permanently from **Parent Mode**.

---

## 4. Hardware Permissions (USB & Serial)

Monster Lab includes an optional **Hardware Companion**. In this release it runs a built-in virtual simulator on every platform, including the web build.
- **No Device Access**: The app does not open USB or serial ports and requests no USB, OTG, or Web Serial permissions.
- **Zero Network Transmission**: Simulated hardware data stays on the device and is never relayed to the internet or external servers.

---

## 5. Compliance with Child Protection Standards

### Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
Monster Lab complies with COPPA requirements because it does not collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

### Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA)
Monster Lab is safe for deployment in classrooms, makerspaces, and school computer labs without creating student records on external cloud platforms.

---

## 6. Contact and Inquiries

For questions regarding Monster Lab privacy practices or curriculum verification, please visit the project repository or submit an issue in our open-source issue tracker.
